Feature Guide & FAQ

How does this version work?

This is a fully standalone tracker! Click Edit Roster in the sidebar to search the live NFL database and pick your players. Your roster is saved directly to your browser memory, so it will remember your team even if you refresh or close the tab.

What do the flashing dots mean?

  • Static Grey: Idle. None of your tracked games are active.
  • Pulsing Green: Active. A game featuring your player is live.
  • Pulsing Red: Red Zone! A tracked team is inside the 20-yard line.
